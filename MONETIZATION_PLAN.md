# LifeFlow Monetization Plan

## Free
Tasks, Money, Goals, Habits, reminders and basic Coach.

## Pro
Advanced AI Coach, analytics/history, smart reminders, exports and future integrations.

## Pricing test
Start with one simple monthly price and one annual option after beta validation. Do not hard-code a payment secret into the browser.

## Launch sequence
1. Validate repeated weekly use.
2. Identify the two Pro features users request most.
3. Connect a supported payment provider.
4. Add server-side subscription verification/webhooks.
5. Turn on paid checkout.
