LifeFlow Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

This policy explains how LifeFlow handles information when you use our personal productivity application.

Information we handle

  • Account information: email address and authentication identifiers used to create and secure your account.
  • Information you enter: tasks, goals, habits, spending records and related app content you choose to save.
  • Reminder settings: reminder preferences and related information stored by the application or your browser.
  • Subscription information: if you buy a paid plan, subscription status and limited transaction or customer identifiers may be processed by the payment provider and shared with LifeFlow to manage access.
  • Technical information: your browser may process basic technical data needed to load and secure the service.

How information is used

We use information to provide account access, save and display your records, operate app features, manage subscriptions, troubleshoot issues and protect the service.

Storage and service providers

LifeFlow uses Supabase for authentication and database services. Payment providers may process payment details and transaction information when paid subscriptions are available. We do not intend to store full payment-card details in LifeFlow.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by service providers needed to run LifeFlow, or disclosed when required by law or reasonably necessary to protect users and the service.

Retention and security

We retain information for as long as reasonably needed to provide the service, meet legal obligations and resolve disputes. We use service-level security controls, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You can stop using LifeFlow and contact us to ask about access, correction or deletion of information. We may need to verify your identity and retain some records where legally required.

Children

LifeFlow is not designed for children under 13, and they should not create accounts without appropriate legal authorization.

Policy updates and contact
