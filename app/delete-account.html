Request LifeFlow Account Deletion

You can request deletion of your LifeFlow account and associated personal data by emailing our support address from the email address registered to your account.

Email an account deletion request

  1. Use the email address registered to your LifeFlow account.
  2. Use the subject “LifeFlow account deletion request.”
  3. Do not send your password.
  4. We may contact you to verify ownership before processing the request.

We aim to respond within 30 days. Some records may need to be retained where required by law, to resolve disputes, or for legitimate billing/security records. Account deletion does not automatically cancel a paid subscription; request subscription cancellation separately and confirm it with the billing provider.
