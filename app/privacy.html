LifeFlow Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

This policy explains how LifeFlow handles information when you use the LifeFlow web app.

Information we handle

  • Account information: your email address and authentication/session information used to create and secure your account.
  • Information you enter: tasks, expenses, goals, habits, and other content you choose to store in the app.
  • Subscription information: subscription status and related identifiers needed to provide Pro access. Checkout and payment processing are handled by Polar; LifeFlow does not ask you to enter your full payment-card details into the app.
  • Coach requests: if you use the AI Coach, your question and relevant app context may be sent to the AI service configured for that feature to generate a response.

How information is used

We use information to provide sign-in, save and display your data, provide requested features, check subscription status, protect the service, and respond to support requests.

Service providers

LifeFlow relies on third-party services, including Supabase for authentication and database services, Polar for checkout/subscription processing, and an AI service when the AI Coach is used. Those providers process information as needed to provide their services under their own terms and privacy policies.

Storage and security

We use access controls intended to limit access to account data. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Please do not store highly sensitive information in LifeFlow.

Retention and deletion

You can request account and associated data deletion by emailing pharmdaamir2121@gmail.com from your account email and writing “Account deletion request.” Do not include your password. We will verify the request and process it, subject to applicable legal, security, and billing-record requirements. Deleting an account does not necessarily cancel a paid subscription; request cancellation separately and confirm it with the billing provider.

Children

LifeFlow is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly seek to collect their personal information.

Policy changes and contact

We may update this policy as the service changes. Questions or privacy requests: pharmdaamir2121@gmail.com.
