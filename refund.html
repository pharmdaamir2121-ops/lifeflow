LifeFlow Refund Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

This policy applies to payments for LifeFlow digital subscriptions.

Requesting a refund

If you believe you were charged in error, purchased the wrong plan, or experienced a material billing problem, email pharmdaamir2121@gmail.com from your account email. Include the charge date, plan and payment-provider transaction reference. Never email your full card number or security code.

Review of requests

We review refund requests individually and will explain the outcome. Where a refund is approved, it will be issued through the original payment method or payment provider when possible. Processing time depends on the payment provider and bank.

Cancellation

You may request cancellation of a recurring subscription using the cancellation option provided by the payment provider or by contacting us. Cancellation generally prevents future renewals; access may continue until the end of the paid period, as stated at checkout. Cancellation does not automatically refund a previous payment.

Legal rights and provider rules

This policy does not limit any consumer rights that cannot legally be excluded. If your purchase was processed by a third-party payment provider, that provider's applicable payment and refund procedures may also apply.

Contact

For billing, cancellation or refund questions, email pharmdaamir2121@gmail.com.
